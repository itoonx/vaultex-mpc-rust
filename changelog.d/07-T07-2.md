- Outflow monitor: first piece, the chain watcher (`vaultex-monitor watch`). It follows TRON on at least two independent public providers (keyless, built in for mainnet and Nile), scans every height in order from a crash-safe per-provider cursor, detects forks, backs off on rate limits (HTTP 429/403, `Retry-After`) and reports transactions signed by watched addresses plus per-provider health as JSON lines. Receipt matching, alerts and the freeze path are not implemented yet.
