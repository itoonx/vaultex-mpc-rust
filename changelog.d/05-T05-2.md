- **Verifier kernel: strict TRON decoder.** `vaultex-verify` now decodes the exact unsigned TRON transaction a signer is asked to sign and accepts only two shapes: a single native TRX transfer, or a TRC-20 `transfer(address,uint256)` on an allowlisted token with no call value. It refuses memos, extra contracts, an unconfigured permission id, permission updates, unknown, duplicate or out-of-order fields, non-minimal or non-canonical encodings and any other selector; it recomputes the transaction id, requires a byte-equal canonical re-encoding, and bounds expiry (24 h ceiling) and `fee_limit`. Every refusal has a named negative test. A fuzz target skeleton and seed corpus are included; the fuzz campaign has not been run yet.
