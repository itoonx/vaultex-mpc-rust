- Supply chain: new `supply-chain/` store for cargo-vet. It imports the google, zcash, mozilla, bytecode-alliance and isrg audit sets, and maps their crypto criteria onto a local `crypto-reviewed` criterion. That criterion deliberately does not imply `safe-to-deploy`. Every current crate starts as an exemption, which means not yet audited and never counts as a crypto review. The crypto crates that no import covers are listed for their own audits before the M7 gate.
- Dependencies: `deny.toml` is no longer a draft. Unsound advisories now count anywhere in the graph. OpenSSL and native-tls are banned (rustls only). The kernel and Guard crypto crates may each appear in only one version. The licence allow-list was checked against today's full dependency graph.
