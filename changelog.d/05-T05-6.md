- **Verifier kernel: signed, hash-chained receipts.** `vaultex-verify` now records every decision, accept or refusal, as an Ed25519-signed receipt that names the digest of the previous one, in an fsync'd, locked, append-only log that is verified in full on every open (a torn last write is truncated; any other damage refuses to start). A new offline verifier, `vaultex-receipts verify <LOG> --key <HEX>`, checks a log or a segment from an anchored checkpoint with strict Ed25519 (canonical S, no small-order keys or R) and ten ordered log rules, and prints its head for anchoring. Draft `RECEIPT_V1` vectors (27 accept and refuse cases, from a second implementation) live in `crates/vaultex-verify/vectors/RECEIPT_V1/`.
