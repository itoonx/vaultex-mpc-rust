- Outflow monitor: receipt matching and alerts (`vaultex-monitor watch --config monitor.json`). Every transaction the float signs must match a Guard accept receipt for the same transaction, wallet and network within a grace period; the monitor reads the Guard's receipt log with its own verifier (pinned key, strict signatures, hash chain, anchored head) and re-checks transaction shape, signing permission, its own per-asset caps (per transaction, rolling 24 h amount and count) and destinations (allowlist, or new-destination pages). It alerts on allowance-raising calls, on non-zero TRC-20 allowances read every minute, and on `transferFrom` spends of the float's tokens. Alerts are printed and journaled; the freeze path is not implemented yet.
- New `vaultex-monitor refill-check`: before a refill, it requires every allowance from the float to be 0 at solidified height on every provider, the balance plus the refill to stay under the float's ceiling, the receipt history to verify, and every severity-1 alert and unmatched transaction to be closed by a record. Exit 0 only on PASS.
