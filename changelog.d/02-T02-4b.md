- Dependencies: async-nats moved from 0.38 to 0.47 (owner-approved). Its old copy of rustls-webpki (0.102) has left the lockfile, and the four advisory exceptions that covered it are removed. The NATS call sites compile unchanged, and a TLS connection still uses the complete rustls client configuration we build. The NATS mTLS loader now reads PEM files with the decoder in rustls-pki-types, so the unmaintained rustls-pemfile is gone along with its exception. The gateway builds prometheus without default features, which drops protobuf 2 and its exception.
- Supply chain: the four remaining advisory exceptions and the three licence exceptions are now signed off by the owner and keep their expiry dates. The cargo-vet exemptions follow the new lockfile.
