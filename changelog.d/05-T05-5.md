- **Verifier kernel: velocity caps and circuit breaker.** `vaultex-verify` now charges every intent it agrees to against sliding-window limits (amount, count and fee per wallet and asset), idempotently per intent, through a `VelocityStore` trait whose file implementation fsyncs each charge before answering, holds an exclusive lock, and on restart truncates only a torn last record and refuses to open on any other damage. A breaker pauses the signer on a burst of suspicious refusals, a cap hit, a clock rollback or a freeze, and the pause survives restarts; lifting it with an M-of-N admin order waits on the policy-distribution decision (ADR-11). A calibration helper sizes each signer's cap from the group budget (`⌊t·G/n⌋`, with the `⌈·⌉` variant) and bounds the worst-case outflow when some signers do not enforce. `kill -9` crash tests check that no acknowledged charge is lost and none is counted twice.
