- **Specs: `INTENT_V1` and `RECEIPT_V1` RFC drafts.** `specs/INTENT_V1.md` specifies the transfer intent a signer derives from the exact unsigned transaction, its canonical BCS encoding and digest, and the strict TRON decoding procedure: every check in order, with its refusal code. `specs/RECEIPT_V1.md` specifies signed, hash-chained decision receipts, the receipt log, the writer rules and offline verification, including one strict Ed25519 verification rule. New `INTENT_V1` vectors (146 accept and refuse cases, with boundary and precedence pairs) sit next to the existing `RECEIPT_V1` vectors in `crates/vaultex-verify/vectors/`. Both RFCs are `DRAFT`; the comment window has not opened.
